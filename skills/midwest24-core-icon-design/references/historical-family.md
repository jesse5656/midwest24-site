The approved launcher family uses a dimensional navy outer shield, shaded
royal-blue/navy field with an upper-left cyan highlight, subtle central ridge,
smoothly curved white inset, and dimensional white pictogram. Flat-cyan fields
and kinked polygon inner shields are rejected treatments, not the historical
standard. The October 29 sheet is a raster approval family; do not
replace it with monochrome line art, flat geometric shields, generic system
icons, unrelated clip art, or duplicate product marks.

The live product image names are not design authority by themselves. Always
compare a complete contact sheet with the approval artifacts before publishing.
