# Midwest24 Archive Knowledge Model

Version: 0.1.0

Status:
Foundational Draft

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Purpose

This document defines the conceptual knowledge model for Midwest24 Archive.

Archive is not designed to be a traditional document management system.

Archive is designed to become Enterprise Knowledge Infrastructure.

Documents are only one form of knowledge.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Vision

The purpose of Archive is to preserve, relate, retrieve, and continuously improve organizational knowledge.

Knowledge should compound rather than disappear.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Core Principle

The fundamental unit of Archive is not a document.

The fundamental unit is a Knowledge Object.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Knowledge Objects

Version 1 should recognize the following object types.

### Observation

A captured fact, event, or insight.

### Question

A problem requiring investigation.

### Hypothesis

A proposed explanation awaiting validation.

### Evidence

Information supporting or challenging a hypothesis.

### Decision

A recorded decision and its rationale.

### Person

An individual connected to knowledge.

### Organization

A company, team, customer, or institution.

### Project

A bounded body of work.

### System

A business or technical system.

### Process

A repeatable workflow.

### Framework

A reusable methodology.

### Paper

A structured research artifact.

### Document

Supporting material or evidence.

### Task

Work requiring completion.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Relationships

Knowledge objects derive value through relationships.

Examples include:

Observation → Question

Question → Hypothesis

Hypothesis → Evidence

Evidence → Paper

Paper → Framework

Framework → System

System → Process

Process → Organization

Decision → Project

Document → Evidence

## Product Principle

Archive should organize knowledge before it organizes files.

Files support knowledge.

Knowledge drives organizations.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Version 1 Goal

The first production release should demonstrate a complete knowledge loop:

1. Capture an Observation.

2. Associate it with a Project.

3. Associate it with a Person.

4. Promote it to a Question.

5. Attach Evidence.

6. Record a Decision.

7. Retrieve the complete knowledge chain.

Version 1 succeeds when organizational knowledge becomes discoverable, understandable, and reusable.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Long-Term Vision

Midwest24 Archive becomes the enterprise operating memory for organizations.

Rather than storing documents, Archive preserves capability.
