# Midwest24 Archive MVP Knowledge Loop

Version: 0.1.0

Status:
Foundational Draft

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Purpose

This document defines the first complete product loop for Midwest24 Archive.

The MVP should prove that Archive can capture, relate, retrieve, and reuse knowledge.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## MVP Objective

Build the smallest usable version of Archive that proves one idea:

Organizational knowledge can be captured as structured knowledge objects rather than unmanaged files.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Core Loop

1. Create an Observation.

2. Associate the Observation with a Project.

3. Associate the Observation with a Person.

4. Promote the Observation into a Question.

5. Attach Evidence.

6. Record a Decision.

7. Retrieve the complete knowledge chain.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Required MVP Objects

Observation

Question

Evidence

Decision

Person

Project

Document

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Required MVP Relationships

Observation belongs to Project.

Observation involves Person.

Observation generates Question.

Question is supported by Evidence.

Decision resolves or advances Question.

Document may support Evidence.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## MVP Success Criteria

The MVP succeeds when a user can:

- capture a useful observation
- connect it to real business context
- attach supporting evidence
- record a decision
- retrieve the full chain later
- understand why the decision was made

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Non-Goals

The MVP does not include:

- full ERP
- full CRM
- accounting
- advanced AI agents
- complex graph visualization
- public SaaS onboarding
- customer billing
- mobile app
- multi-tenant commercial deployment

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Guiding Principle

Build the smallest complete knowledge system.

Do not build a large incomplete platform.
